The [Google Summer of Code](http://code.google.com/soc/) is a now yearly event
where university students (including PhD students) can work on open source project,
and get payed fairly well (mentors only get a T-shirt :). A few years ago I
was mentor for the [KDE](http://kde.org) project, supervising work on [Strigi-Chemistry](http://neksa.blogspot.com/).
Various open source science projects participate again this year (no, no chemistry
projects, I'm afraid), but a few come close. Here are my tips of this year:

* tip #1: [debian](http://debian.org) java packaging for [taverna](http://taverna.sf.net)
  [jalview](http://www.jalview.org/) [jmol](http://jmol.org) [bioclipse](http://bioclipse.net)
  and [cdk](http://cdk.sf.net)? [http://goo.gl/12iU9](http://goo.gl/12iU9)
* tip #2: pathway information, [OWL](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_Ontology_Language),
  what can you wish for more? [CHEMINF](http://code.google.com/p/semanticchemistry/)
  perhaps? [http://goo.gl/qInJB](http://goo.gl/qInJB)
* tip #3: Nescent has the [jmol](http://jmol.org) protein evolution again. Great
  idea, cool graphics [http://goo.gl/c0r0j](http://goo.gl/c0r0j)
* tip #4: how much I love the SWT-based [Cytoscape](http://www.cytoscape.org/)
  in [Bioclipse](http://bioclipse.net), cytoscape in HTML5 rocks [http://goo.gl/jbgWY](http://goo.gl/jbgWY)
* tip #5: #java [BioJava3](http://www.biojava.org/wiki/BioJava3_project) AA
  psysico-chemical prop calculations... sounds like [http://pharmbio.org](http://pharmbio.org)
  to me [http://goo.gl/EG7tG](http://goo.gl/EG7tG)
* tip #6: comptox in Orange with [AZOrange](https://github.com/AZCompTox/AZOrange)
  [http://goo.gl/XS0vf](http://goo.gl/XS0vf) with perhaps room for [CDK](http://cdk.sf.net)
  and [Cinfony](http://code.google.com/p/cinfony/)
