Thanx to the work of [Onkar Shinde](https://launchpad.net/~onkarshinde), the
[Debian](http://www.debian.org/) package for [CDK](http://cdk.sf.net/) has been
updated from 1.0.2 (in [Squeeze](http://wiki.debian.org/DebianSqueeze)) to 1.2.7
in [unstable](http://wiki.debian.org/DebianUnstable). The popularity content
shows some [140 installations](http://qa.debian.org/popcon.php?package=cdk):

![](/blog/assets/images/cdkPopCon.png)

BTW, [Jmol](http://packages.debian.org/search?keywords=jmol) 12 is also available
as Debian package now!
